RetroPlay

Classic consoles, right in your browser.

Your games

    ROMs stay on this device. They're stored in your browser so you don't have to pick them again, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Game saves are kept in your browser too.

    Powered by EmulatorJS (GPL-3.0) and libretro cores (FCEUmm, Snes9x, Gambatte, mGBA, SMS Plus, Genesis Plus GX, Beetle PCE/NeoPop/Cygne, Stella, ProSystem). No games are included.
